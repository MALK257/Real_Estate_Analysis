# Real Estate Portfolio Analysis

## Overview
An analysis of a real estate portfolio of **3,000 properties** across five property types (**Apartment, Condo, Townhouse, Single Family, Multi-Family**), handled by five listing agents and classified into three market statuses (**Sold, For Sale, Pending**). The goal is to monitor market status and identify where unsold inventory is concentrated.

Built using **Power BI**.

## Business Problem
Only **34.83%** of properties are **Sold**. The remaining **1,955 properties (65.17%)** are **For Sale (957)** or **Pending (998)**, with no clear view of where this unsold inventory is concentrated.

## Business Questions & Answers

1. **What is the overall market status?**  
   **Sold** is **1,045 (34.83%)**, **Pending** is **998 (33.27%)**, and **For Sale** is **957 (31.90%)** out of **3,000** properties.

2. **How much inventory is unsold?**  
   **1,955 properties (65.17%)**.

3. **How do Pending and For Sale compare?**  
   **Pending** exceeds **For Sale** by **41 properties** (**998 vs. 957**).

4. **How are properties spread across the days breakdown?**  
   Fairly evenly across the **0-30**, **31-60**, **61-90**, and **Over 90 Days** buckets, with no single bucket dominating.

5. **Which property types are largest?**  
   **Apartment** is the largest and **Multi-Family** is the smallest, with **Condo**, **Townhouse**, and **Single Family** in descending order between them.

---

## Key Insights
* The portfolio is split almost evenly across the three statuses (**31.90% to 34.83%**), with only about one-third **Sold**.
* **Pending (998)** is almost equal in size to **Sold (1,045)**; converting all **Pending** properties would raise **Sold** to **2,043 (68.10%)**.

---

## Recommendations
1. Prioritize closing the **998 Pending** properties: every **100** converted adds about **3.3 percentage points** to the **Sold** share.
2. Review **For Sale** listings in the **Over 90 Days** bucket first for pricing and marketing adjustments.
3. Compare **Sold** results across the five listing agents to identify effective practices.
4. Break down status by property type to locate where unsold inventory concentrates.
